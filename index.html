How it works

  • Soil moisture + rain forecast: forecast rain is added to current moisture, so zones that rain will refill are skipped.
  • Min-Heap: every thirsty zone is pushed into a priority queue ordered by effective moisture.
  • Greedy: the driest zone is popped and watered first until the water budget runs out.
  • Graph + Dijkstra: the pipe network gives the shortest route from the tank; longer routes lose more water in the pipes.
